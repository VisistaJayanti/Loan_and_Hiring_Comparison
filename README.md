# Loan_and_Hiring_Comparison

This project is about creating a model that predicts the hiring rate of an individual based on loan history and financial commitments. This project solely focuses on financial and socio-economical aspects regarding loans and hiring. The parameters chosen for this project will refer to the ones that are depdendent on hiring process and are co-related with loan history. 

The project focuses on collecting socio-economic parameters which provide a qualitative analysis for an individual getting hired. 
These include the following:
1) Age of an individual (Relevance - An individual aged 20 with loans may have a higher chance of getting hired than a 50 year old invidiual with loans)
2) Gender of an individual (Relevance - In some organizations, males are given a higher chance of being hired than females)
3) Marital status (Relevance - There is a difference in stability when an individual pays the loans alone or if he/she has a family dependent)
4) Credit score (Relevance - Credit score can determine the person's financial stability and his/her capability to repay the loans)
5) Type of loans (Relevance - Some loans are more urgent than others), education level (Relevance - qualification of the individual)
6) Ehtnicity or nationality (Relevance - There is often discrimination when it comes to minortiy classes or individuals from under-developed countries)
7) Location of living (Relevance - A person earning and living in urban area has more stability to repay the loan than a person in suburbs or rural area).
